# Art sources and placeholders

UI/Summoner/*.png are original geometric placeholders authored by Tools/Create-Placeholders.ps1 for this project. They may be replaced with final art later.

The living spirit wolf uses original generated directional art under `Things/Pawn/Animal/SpiritWolf/`. The source sheet is kept under `ArtSource/SpiritWolf/` and the deterministic crop/rescale step is `Tools/Build-SpiritWolfTextures.ps1`. The final design is a charcoal wolf with emerald eyes, soul markings, and a restrained green aura. Evolution currently uses the same silhouette with stronger gameplay effects and is identified in the contract tab and health status.

No RimWorld of Magic or other third-party art is included.
